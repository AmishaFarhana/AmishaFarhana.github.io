About Me

Your Photo Here

I'm a data enthusiast, community builder, and lifelong learner currently pursuing my MS in Business Analytics at Cal State East Bay. Before grad school, I worked as a KYC/AML Analyst at JPMorgan Chase, where I first discovered the power of data-driven decision-making. Sifting through complex financial data to detect risk patterns taught me that the right question, paired with the right data, can change everything.

That experience lit a fire in me. I wanted to go deeper — not just analyze data, but build systems that learn, predict, and optimize. So I dove headfirst into machine learning, big data engineering, and statistical modeling. Today, my work spans from building ML pipelines and recommendation engines to designing dashboards that tell stories stakeholders actually care about.

Outside of code and classrooms, you'll find me mentoring peers, leading student organizations, and writing on LinkedIn about the intersection of data, leadership, and personal growth. I believe that what we build matters — but so does how we show up for the people around us.
